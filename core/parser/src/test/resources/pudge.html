Trivia

  • Pudge was one of the original Dota heroes, adapted from Warcraft III custom maps by Eul.
  • His iconic voice line "Fresh meat!" is one of the most recognized in gaming history.
  • Pudge is statistically one of the most played heroes in Dota 2 history across all skill brackets.
  • The character design is inspired by traditional horror genre butchers and flesh-golems.

Background

Among the flesh-piles of the charnel houses of Strygwyr the Bloodseeker lies a horror born of pure carnage. Pudge the Butcher is a being of singular will and singular hunger, his body a shrine to consumption. Each soul he devours feeds the flesh — and the flesh is never sated. He wanders the battlefield not as a servant of chaos but as an independent contractor of death, dragging the freshly fallen back to whatever dark alcove he calls home this particular evening.
