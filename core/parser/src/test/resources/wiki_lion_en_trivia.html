Trivia[]

  • In the transition from DotA to Dota 2, the names of two of Lion's abilities were changed. "Impale" changed to Earth Spike and "Voodoo" changed to Hex.
  • Lion's rival response towards Witch Doctor ▶️ "All we need now is a wardrobe." is a reference to the second book in The Chronicles of Narnia book series by C.S. Lewis, titled The Lion, the Witch, and the Wardrobe.
