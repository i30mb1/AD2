About Dota is a Dota 2 companion with hero guides, item details and mini-games for people who play it.

HEROES
• Every hero in numbers: health, mana, damage, armor and speed at level 1, 15 and 25
• Abilities with damage, cooldown and mana cost, the innate ability, talents, Aghanim's Scepter and Shard
• Who the hero beats and who beats them, and what is bought by mid game — from OpenDota statistics
• Hero voice lines in English and Russian that you can play right in the app
• Tips, patch changes and the hero's lore

GUIDES
• Win rate, matches, pro picks and bans
• Win rate by rank — from Herald to Divine
• Skill build popular in pro matches
• Items and timings: start, early, mid and late game

ITEMS
• Every item with its description, build and cost
• Which heroes the item suits, and fun facts

TOURNAMENTS AND STREAMS
• Live matches and today's schedule
• Dota 2 streams in English and Russian

MINI-GAMES
• Quiz Show: mana costs, hero voices, recipes, who has more — with bets, 50/50 and an «I'm first!» button
• Daily challenge: 10 questions, the same for everyone, and a streak of days in a row
• Mid: a lane duel — last hit and deny creeps against a bot or a friend
• Invoker: invoke as many spells as you can in a minute
• Tic-tac-toe 9×9: nine boards where your move picks the board for your opponent
• Play together on one Wi-Fi network — up to 6 players
• Levels, experience and your friends' records right on the game cards

The app is available in English and Russian.
